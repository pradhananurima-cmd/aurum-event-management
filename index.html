    The department

    Built by students, for students

    The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata runs a four-year B.Tech programme centred on machine learning, deep learning, data science and their applications. The department maintains dedicated AIML and IoT laboratories and works closely with the IETE Student Forum and the institute's innovation cell.

    Every event on this platform is planned by a student committee under faculty guidance and is open to all branches unless stated otherwise.
